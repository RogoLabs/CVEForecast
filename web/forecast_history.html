What we predicted, and when

Every run records the forecast it produced. As months complete, those records turn into a public accuracy trail: not just how good the models look on historical backtest, but how good the numbers on the front page actually turned out to be. A forecast that quietly improves once the answer is known is not a forecast, so this page is built from snapshots taken before the outcome was available.

Data revision

CVE records keep arriving after a month ends, so the count for a month depends on when you looked. Each run records what every recent month looked like at that moment, which is the only way to measure the effect — it cannot be reconstructed after the fact.
